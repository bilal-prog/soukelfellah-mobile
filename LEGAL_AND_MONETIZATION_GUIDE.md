## 1. 🛡️ Legal & Tax Compliance Checklist (Morocco Context)

### A. Neutral Intermediary Status (*Hébergeur / Tiers Intermédiaire*)
- **Terms of Service (CGU):** Explicitly state that Souk El Fellah acts strictly as a neutral hosting platform connecting agricultural buyers, sellers, and service providers.
- **No Direct Involvement:** Declare that all sale agreements, price negotiations, item inspections, payments, and deliveries occur directly between users outside the platform. The platform does not hold funds in escrow, act as a commercial broker, or guarantee seller inventory.

### B. Tax Structure Progression (DGI Morocco)
- **Phase 1: Free Platform Launch (Zero Revenue)**
  - If the service is free and earns zero revenue, there are no immediate transaction tax obligations for platform operations.
- **Phase 2: Initial Monetization (Auto-Entrepreneur Status)**
  - Once revenue starts (via ad networks, boosted listings, or sponsors), register under the **Auto-Entrepreneur** status ([rnae.ma](https://rnae.ma)).
  - Rates: **1%** tax on commercial sales, **2%** on service provisions up to annual thresholds (200,000 MAD for services / 500,000 MAD for commercial trade).
- **Phase 3: Business Scaling (SARL / SARL AU)**
  - Once annual revenue scales or you take outside investment, incorporate a **SARL** (Société à Responsabilité Limitée) or **SARL AU** (Single-member LLC) to separate personal liability from company assets.

### C. Data Privacy & Compliance (CNDP — Law n° 09-08)
- **CNDP Declaration:** Submit a standard online data processing declaration (*Déclaration préalable de traitement des données à caractère personnel*) at [cndp.ma](https://www.cndp.ma) for collecting phone numbers, names, and user locations.
- **Account & Data Deletion:** Maintain an accessible account deletion option (`/delete-account` route) in accordance with CNDP Law 09-08 and Google Play Store policies.
- **Privacy Policy:** Keep the Privacy Policy clear and accessible on the platform.

### D. Content Moderation & Fraud Prevention
- **Prohibited Listings:** Define banned items in your CGU (stolen machinery, restricted pesticides, protected species, or fraudulent offers).
- **Reporting Mechanism:** Maintain a "Report Listing" (*Signaler*) button on listing pages to allow users to flag suspicious content, protecting platform owners under intermediary liability rules.

### E. Intellectual Property (OMPIC)
- Register the brand name **Souk El Fellah (سوق الفلاح)** and official logo with **OMPIC** (*Office Marocain de la Propriété Industrielle et Commerciale* — [ompic.ma](http://www.ompic.ma)) to protect the platform's brand identity in Morocco.

- **Banner Ads:** ~$0.20 – $0.60 per 1,000 impressions
- **Interstitial / Native Ads:** ~$1.00 – $2.50 per 1,000 impressions

### Revenue Stream 2: Featured / Boosted Listings (*Annonces à la une*) — **Highest Margin**
In classifieds platforms (like Avito or Dubizzle), internal listing boosts account for 70%+ of overall profit.
- **Pricing Model:** Charge sellers **20 MAD to 50 MAD** ($2 – $5) to feature their listing (tractor, livestock, harvest) at the top of search results for 7 days.
- **Revenue Projections:**
  - 50 boosted listings / month @ 30 MAD = **1,500 MAD / mo**
  - 300 boosted listings / month @ 30 MAD = **9,000 MAD / mo**

---

### Revenue Stream 3: Direct B2B Agricultural Sponsor Banners
Agriculture in Morocco involves high-ticket commercial transactions (solar pumps, tractors, fertilizers, seed companies, irrigation supplies).
- **Direct Sponsor Banners:** Sell top homepage banner spots directly to agricultural suppliers for a fixed monthly sponsorship fee.
- **Rates in Morocco:** ~**1,500 MAD to 5,000 MAD / month** per sponsor brand.
- **Calculation:** 3 direct sponsors @ 3,000 MAD = **9,000 MAD / month**.
